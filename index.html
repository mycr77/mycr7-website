How to Participate

  1. Open the official Telegram bot.
  2. Join the official Telegram channel.
  3. Submit your Instagram username and wait for admin approval.
  4. Register your Solana public wallet address.
  5. Invite friends using your referral link and complete the daily quiz.

Referral Rules

A valid referral must join the official Telegram channel and have their Instagram account approved by an admin. If they are not a member at final settlement, the provisional referral reward is deducted. Rejoining before settlement restores eligibility, but does not create a second referral reward.
